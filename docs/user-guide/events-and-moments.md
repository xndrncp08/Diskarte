# Events and Moments

## Events

**See events:** the **Events** row in a Tambayan's channel list shows how many are coming up (or
**LIVE** when one has started).

**RSVP:** open an event and press **Interested**. If the event has a limit and it's full, you'll see
that. With notifications on, Diskarte reminds you 15 minutes before it starts.

**Add to your calendar:** the **Calendar** button downloads a file your phone or computer calendar can
import.

**Create an event (moderators):**

1. Open **Events** → schedule a new event.
2. Give it a name, description, start and end time, and either a voice channel or another location.
3. Optional: set a capacity (how many people can say they're coming).
4. Optional: **Repeat** weekly for 4, 8 or 12 weeks — Diskarte creates each week as its own event,
   so you can change or cancel one without affecting the others.

Events in a private channel are only visible to people who can see that channel.

## Moments

Moments are short updates — text, a photo, or both — that disappear after **24 hours**. Find them on
**Home**.

- **Share a moment:** choose who sees it: **all your friends** or **only your favourites**. You can
  have up to 10 live moments.
- **Mute** someone to stop seeing their moments without unfriending them.
- **Report** a moment that breaks the rules; the Diskarte team reviews it.
- **Delete** your own moment anytime from its options.

Mark friends as favourites from your Friends list.

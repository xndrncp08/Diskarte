# Notifications, privacy and safety

## Notifications

Open **Settings → Notifications**:

- **Desktop notifications** for new DMs while Diskarte is open in a tab.
- **Sounds** — the little 8-bit beeps. Change the volume from the speaker button next to your name.
- **Quiet hours** — no sounds, rings or alerts between times you choose.
- **Pause for 1 hour** — a quick break.
- **This device** (push notifications) — get alerts even when Diskarte is closed. Choose which kinds:
  DMs, mentions, replies, friend requests, invitations, calls, events, moderation and security.
  **Previews** are off by default, so your lock screen just says "Diskarte"; turn them on to see who
  wrote and what.

On iPhone, install Diskarte to your home screen first (see [Getting started](getting-started.md)).
Push notifications also depend on your phone's focus modes and battery settings.

**Per Tambayan:** **Only @mentions**, **Nothing**,
or **Mute Server** (until you unmute, or **Mute for 1 hour** / **Mute for 8 hours**). This lives in the
Tambayan's menu under Notification Settings. Right-click a channel →
**Mute Channel**.

## Privacy

- **Who can add you:** Settings → **Privacy & Safety** → everyone, only people in a Tambayan with you,
  or nobody.
- **DMs** only come from friends.
- **Friend nicknames and notes** you add are visible only to you.
- **Favourites** are private too.
- Seen receipts in DMs can't be turned off yet.

## Blocking

Right-click someone → **Block**. They can't message you or send you friend requests, and to them it
looks like your account doesn't exist. Unblock from the same menu.

## Protect your account

- Use a strong, unique password.
- Turn on **two-factor sign-in**: Settings → My Account → Two-factor, then scan the code with an
  authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password…). After that you'll enter a 6-digit code
  when you sign in. Keep your authenticator safe — losing it means asking an administrator for help.
- **Settings → Devices & Sessions → sign out everywhere** if you used a shared computer.

## Your data

- **Download everything:** Settings → Storage & Data → **Request all of my data** gives you a file with
  everything your account wrote or set.
- **Delete your account:** Settings → My Account → **Danger zone**. Type your username to confirm.
  Your files and private data are deleted. If you own Tambayans, they pass to their most senior
  member (or are deleted if nobody else is in them). This can't be undone.

## Report a problem

Settings → **Report a problem**: choose what kind of problem, describe what happened, and send. The
Diskarte team reviews every report.

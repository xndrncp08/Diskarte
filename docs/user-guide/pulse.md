# Try the new Diskarte

**Diskarte Pulse** is an optional new look for Diskarte. It brings your Spaces, conversations and calls
into one canvas. Classic remains the default. Pulse becomes available when this Diskarte's operator
opens the opt-in preview; this guide does not mean the preview is enabled on every deployment.

## Turn Pulse on or off

1. Open **Settings → Appearance**.
2. Turn on **Try the new Diskarte**, when the switch is available.
3. To return, turn the switch off, or choose **Back to Classic** from **You**.

Your choice belongs to this browser. It does not move or copy your account, messages or community data.
Switching keeps the current conversation and active call in the app. Your other preferences and
notifications keep their existing settings. Switching is different from reloading the page.

## Find your way around

On a computer, the **Spaces navigator** brings Home, People and your Spaces together. A Space is your
Tambayan; its rooms are the same channels you already use. Open a Space to see its rooms and community
overview. **Jump to…** or **Ctrl/⌘ K** opens the navigation search.

On a phone, the four main destinations are:

| Destination | What you find |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your starting point, friends who are around and recent activity |
| **People** | Conversations, Friends and **Start a Hangout** |
| **Spaces** | Your Tambayans and links to explore or add a Space |
| **You** | Your profile, status and settings |

Conversations use the full phone screen. **Back** takes a DM to People and a room to its Space's
overview. Existing conversation links still open directly. In a browser, use its normal back gesture;
the installed app also offers an edge swipe. The bottom navigation gets out of the way while the
keyboard is open.

## Conversations and context

Messages read like a transcript, with names and times beside the conversation. Reply, react, edit
and other message actions are still available. Long-press a message or navigation row on a touch
screen to open its actions. With a keyboard, use **Shift F10** for a focused row's context menu.

The **Context** panel brings members, pinned messages and room details beside the conversation.
On a phone it opens as a sheet. Close it with **Close**, Escape or the background outside the sheet.

## Calls in Pulse

Voice rooms open on a Stage with each person's name and microphone state. Call buttons have names
and show their current state. While you browse elsewhere, the live call strip takes you back to the
Stage and gives you quick mute and leave controls. On a phone, open the Stage for the full set of
controls.

If the browser blocks sound, choose **Turn on sound**. If microphone access is blocked, allow it in
your browser's site settings, then choose **Try again**. Reconnecting and weak-connection notices
explain the current call state. See [Calls](calls.md) for devices, screen sharing and troubleshooting.

## Accessibility and feedback

Pulse follows your existing accessibility preferences, including reduced motion, text size and
contrast settings. Controls support keyboard focus, and call status is described in words as well
as colour. Phone and screen-reader behaviour can vary by browser; report problems through
**Settings → Report a problem** and include your device, browser and whether you installed the app.

You can return to Classic while the preview is being improved. See [known issues](../known-limitations.md).

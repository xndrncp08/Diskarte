# Tambayans

## Create a Tambayan

1. Click **Add a server** (the **+** on the left rail; on phones, from Home).
2. Give it a name and short description.
3. Pick a template — Gaming crew, Study group, Creator community, Friend group, or Organization / team.
   Every template comes with a general chat and a voice channel; some add channels for resources and
   discussions.
4. Done — you're the **owner**. Change anything later in **Server settings**.

## Invite friends

- Right-click your Tambayan (long-press on phones) → **Invite people**, or **Copy invite link**.
- Choose how long the link works (30 minutes up to "never") and how many people can use it.
- Friends open the link, sign in and join. You can also send the invite straight to a friend's DMs.
- Want strangers to find you? Admins can list the Tambayan in **Explore** with a category (Gaming,
  Study, Creators, Friends, Music, Tech, Anime, Sports, Other).

People who were banned can't rejoin with any link.

## Set up channels

Right-click the channel list → **Create channel**:

- **Text** for chatting, **Voice** for calls, **Forum** for titled posts with their own threads.
- Group channels into categories and drag them to reorder.
- Edit a channel to add a topic, **slow mode** (how often each person may post), **read-only**
  (only moderators post), or **verified accounts only**.
- Admins can make a channel **private** and choose which people or roles can see it.

## Roles and helpers

Every member has a rank:

| Rank | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Member | Chat, react, join calls, make invites, RSVP |
| Moderator | Also pin and delete messages, time out, warn, kick and ban members, manage channels, see the audit log |
| Admin | Also change settings, ranks and private channels |
| Owner | Everything, including deleting or transferring the Tambayan |

Change someone's rank or give them a **custom role** (a coloured label) by right-clicking them in the
member list. Roles can unlock private channels.

## Run your community

Open **Server settings** from the Tambayan's menu. Admins see every tab; moderators see the audit log,
bans and invites.

| Tab | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Name, icon, description, Explore listing, **rules**, **welcome message**, **levels**, export settings |
| Roles | Create coloured roles |
| Bantay-Bayan | Turn on auto-moderation: spam, scam links, hate speech, explicit content, and your own blocked words |
| Audit log | See who did what (joins, kicks, bans, deleted messages, auto-mod blocks…) |
| Bans | See bans and lift them |
| Invites | See and revoke invite links |
| Emoji | Upload up to 50 custom emoji |
| Soundboard | Upload short MP3 clips for calls |
| Webhooks | Let other apps post into a channel |
| Announcements | Post a message automatically every day or every week |
| Support | Add GCash/Maya numbers and grant supporter badges |

**Rules:** if you write rules, members must accept them before they can post. Change the rules and
everyone is asked again.

**Welcome messages:** greet new members automatically. Use `{user}` for their name and `{server}` for
the Tambayan's name, e.g. `Mabuhay {user}! Welcome sa {server}.`

**Levels:** members earn points for chatting (at most once a minute, so spamming doesn't help). A
leaderboard shows the most active members. You can reset for a new season.

## Keep it safe

Right-click a member for:

- **Timeout** (60 seconds to a week) — they can read but not talk.
- **Warn** — they see your note once.
- **Kick** or **Ban** (with a reason).
- In a voice channel: **mute**, **deafen**, **disconnect** or **move** them.

You can only act on people with a lower rank than yours. Everything is written to the audit log.

## Leave or delete

- **Leave server** from the Tambayan's menu. Owners must hand the Tambayan to someone else or delete it
  first.
- **Delete** (owner only) removes all channels and messages permanently.

# Report a problem

## In Diskarte (signed in)

**Settings → Report a problem**:

1. Choose what kind of problem it is: something's broken, it's slow or laggy, account or sign-in, or
   something else.
2. Describe what you were doing, what you expected and what happened (at least 10 characters).
3. Send. The page you were on and your browser type are attached so the team can reproduce it.

The Diskarte team reviews every report. You can send up to five reports an hour.

**Don't include passwords, codes or private conversations** in a report.

## Problems with these docs

Use **Suggest an improvement** at the bottom of any docs page. When you're signed in it goes to the
same review queue; otherwise it opens a prefilled issue on GitHub.

## Security issues

Please report vulnerabilities privately, not in a public issue — see the project's
[security policy](../../SECURITY.md).

## Inappropriate content or people

- In a Tambayan, tell its moderators; they can delete messages, time out, warn, kick or ban.
- **Block** anyone who bothers you.
- **Report** a Moment from its options menu.

## Before you report

Check [Known issues](../known-limitations.md) and the other help pages — your problem may already have
a fix.

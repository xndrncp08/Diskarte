# Frequently asked questions

## Is Diskarte free?

Yes. Diskarte is open source and can run on free tiers of its hosting services. There are no paid
plans inside the app; Tambayan "support" badges and GCash/Maya numbers are just for communities that
want to thank their supporters, and payments happen outside Diskarte.

## Do I need to download an app?

No. Diskarte works in your browser. You can also install it to your home screen like an app — see
[Create an account and install](../user-guide/getting-started.md#install-diskarte-on-your-phone).

## Who can message me?

Only your friends can start a DM with you. You choose who can send you friend requests in
**Settings → Privacy & Safety**: everyone, only people who share a Tambayan with you, or nobody.
Block someone to stop them contacting you completely.

## Who can see my messages?

- Messages in a Tambayan channel: members of that Tambayan (and, for a private channel, only the
  people and roles allowed in it).
- DMs: only the people in that conversation.
- Moments: your friends, or only your favourites if you choose.

Messages are protected in transit and by access rules on the server. They are **not** end-to-end
encrypted.

## How many Tambayans can I join?

Up to 100.

## Can I edit or delete a message?

Yes, your own. Hover (or long-press) the message and choose **Edit** or **Delete**. Edited messages
show "(edited)"; you and the Tambayan's moderators can see earlier versions.

## Why did my message disappear right after I sent it?

The Tambayan's auto-moderation probably blocked it (spam, a suspicious link or a blocked word). Ask a
moderator if you think it was a mistake.

## Does Diskarte work on slow or prepaid connections?

Yes. Turn on **Low-data mode** in **Settings → Storage & Data**. Messages you send while offline wait
in a queue and go out when you reconnect. See [Connection problems](connection.md).

## Can I use Diskarte in Filipino?

The main menus and settings can be shown in Filipino: **Settings → Language & Region**. Most other
text is in English for now.

## How do I get my data or delete my account?

**Settings → Storage & Data → Request all of my data** downloads everything your account wrote or
set. **Settings → My Account → Danger zone** deletes your account permanently. See
[Notifications, privacy and safety](../user-guide/notifications-and-privacy.md#your-data).

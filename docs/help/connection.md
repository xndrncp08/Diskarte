# Connection problems

## Messages won't send

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| An 8-bit **offline** bar and messages marked as waiting | You're offline. Keep going — waiting messages send automatically, in order, when you reconnect |
| A message marked as failed with **Retry** | Tap **Retry**. Retrying never creates a duplicate |
| "Slow down — you're sending too fast" | Wait a few seconds |
| "Slow mode — you can send again in …" | The channel limits how often people post |

## Messages from others stop appearing

Diskarte reconnects on its own and fetches anything you missed. If a chat still looks stale:

1. Use **Retry sync** if it appears in the chat.
2. Check that you're still a member of the Tambayan (you may have been removed or a channel made
   private).
3. Reload the page.

**Settings → Advanced → Connection diagnostics** lists recent connection events on this device (no
message content), which helps when you report a problem.

## Voice or video problems

| Problem | Try |
| --- | --- |
| "Couldn't connect to voice" | Check your connection and press **Join** again |
| The browser blocked your microphone or camera | Allow them in the site settings (the lock icon in the address bar). Diskarte only works with media over HTTPS |
| Others can't hear you | Check **Settings → Voice & Video** for the right microphone and use **Mic Test**; make sure you aren't muted or using push to talk |
| You can't hear anyone | Check you aren't **deafened**, the mixer volume, and your output device. After a page reload, press **turn on sound** if it appears |
| Choppy audio or video | Turn your camera off, or turn on **Low-data mode**. The signal bars show latency and packet loss |
| "You're timed out in this server" | A moderator timed you out; it ends on its own |
| Dropped after the page reloaded | Diskarte rejoins the call automatically within 30 seconds |

## Using a weak or expensive connection

- **Low-data mode** (Settings → Storage & Data) loads smaller images, stops GIF autoplay and uses
  lower video quality.
- Audio-only calls use very little data.
- Installed Diskarte keeps its app files on your device, so it opens faster on slow networks.
